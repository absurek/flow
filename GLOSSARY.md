# Flow

Personal record of meetings and the projects discussed in them.

## Language

**Meeting**:
A single timeline post: a titled account of something that happened at a particular time.
_Avoid_: Post, entry, meeting notes

**Project**:
A named body of work tracked across meetings. It has tasks and one notes field.
_Avoid_: Issue, todo, ticket

**Task**:
One checkable line of work on a Project. It is either done or open.
_Avoid_: Bullet, item, todo, issue

**Reference**:
The link from one Meeting to one Project, meaning that Meeting discussed that Project. A Meeting references a given Project at most once.
_Avoid_: Mention, tag, attachment, snapshot
